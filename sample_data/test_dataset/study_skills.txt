Study Skills — synthetic test notes

[Page 1]
Spaced practice means returning to material across multiple study sessions with time between sessions, instead of doing all practice in one sitting. A review schedule should fit the student's available time and should not be changed without their agreement.

[Page 2]
Retrieval practice asks a learner to recall an idea before looking at the answer. Feedback helps the learner compare their response with the material and decide what to review. A single quiz result is limited evidence and should not be treated as a complete measure of ability.

[Page 3]
Worked examples show a sequence of steps for solving a problem. After studying an example, a learner can try a similar problem with less guidance. When a learner makes a mistake, useful feedback identifies the step to reconsider rather than only repeating the final answer.

[Page 4]
Planning note: choose one or two concrete goals for a study session, reserve a short review period, and leave breaks between demanding tasks. This note does not specify a universal schedule for every student.
